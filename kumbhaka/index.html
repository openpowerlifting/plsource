Kumbhaka Breath Hold

This is a training aid for mastering the Kumbhaka Hold, as introduced in the text Yantra Yoga: the Tibetan Yoga of Movement  by Chögyal Namkhai Norbu, p. 140.

Please follow the instructions linked above, under the section "Breathing Method".

The line moving up represents inhalation and a closed hold. The line moving down represents exhalation and an open hold.

You can use the top-left controls or the keyboard left/right arrow buttons to change level. The space bar resets to the start of a fresh inhalation.

Please relax and enjoy!
